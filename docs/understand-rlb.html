Technical reference

How RLB works and what it supports.

This branch is for people who want to understand the product in detail: its 141-system registry, file-format recognition, catalog coverage, workspaces, preservation model, knowledge, readiness rules, source handling, deployment and physically accepted builds.

SYSTEM KNOWLEDGE

141 systems in the RLB System Registry

RLB's content knowledge extends across consoles, handhelds, computers, arcade platforms and specialist engines. In the June 21, 2026 catalog audit, 88 registry systems already had matched catalog/hash data representing 300,402 titles and 906,775 hashes.
