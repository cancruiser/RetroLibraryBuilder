Protect an original handheld SD card

A conservative first workflow for a device whose original storage may be unreliable or difficult to replace.

Keep the original media unchangedDo not delete or reorganize files on the original card just to make it easier for RLB to scan.
Open Device InspectorAllow RLB to examine the connected media and build an evidence-based Device Report.
Confirm the device result makes senseReview the identification and evidence before relying on device-specific knowledge.
Select the protection workflowChoose the intended preservation level and a stable destination with enough free space.
Run Analyze firstReview the preview and reconciliation counts before approving work that establishes content.
Run the preservation operationLeave the source and destination connected while RLB performs the work and verification.
Check the terminal preservation resultVerify that the preservation actually completed. A saved Device Report by itself is not proof of preservation.
Keep the original card until you trust the resultEspecially during Beta, retain the original media until you have confirmed the protection you intended to create.
