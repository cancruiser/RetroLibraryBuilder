RLB CORE

More than an SD-card copier

Retro handhelds can hide important device-specific files, boot assets, configuration, saves, media and content across multiple partitions. RLB is designed to understand the device before deciding what protection is required.

◎

Understand

Inspect storage, boot evidence, firmware characteristics, device signatures and content organization.

◇

Preserve

Establish verified protection instead of assuming a same-named or same-sized file is good enough.

▦

Build

Use verified reusable Library content and known device information when preparing replacement media.

✦

Know

Accumulate reusable knowledge about devices, boards, firmware, DTBs and related hardware.

WORKFLOW

A typical RLB workflow

The goal is simple: understand the original device first, protect its important content, then use that knowledge when it is time to rebuild.

Connect or insert the original mediaStart with the device media you want RLB to understand and protect.
Inspect the deviceDevice Inspector gathers evidence about identity, layout and content.
Review the evidenceCheck what RLB believes the device is and why it reached that conclusion.
Analyze protection requirementsAnalysis determines the work required before changes or copies are made.
Run preservationRLB establishes verified representations and retains content that must remain specific to the preservation.
Use the knowledge laterPreserved evidence and reusable Library content can support future replacement-media and deployment workflows.
