Evidence and provenance

Knowledge should be useful — and traceable.

RLB combines credited source material with its own canonical knowledge layer so device, firmware and hardware evidence can be reused without hiding where that evidence came from.

What can the repository know about?

Knowledge is separated into domains so a device is not reduced to one flat record.

DevicesCanonical devices, aliases, manufacturers and families.
FirmwareKnown firmware families and compatibility relationships.
Boards & HardwareSoCs, hardware profiles and board relationships.
PanelsDisplay evidence that can be critical to safe firmware selection.
DTBsDevice-tree fingerprints, associations and boot evidence.
RecipesReusable handling/deployment knowledge where validated.
MediaDevice recognition imagery and related knowledge media.
Known IssuesRisks and incompatibilities that should remain visible.
EVIDENCE-BASED DEVICE IDENTITY Knowledge informs Device Inspector; it does not replace evidence from the device. A known product name or firmware mention is not enough by itself. Device Inspector compares real source evidence with known relationships and should preserve ambiguity when the evidence is insufficient.
