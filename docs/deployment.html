WORKFLOW

What Deployment does

The exact mechanics vary by firmware, but the accepted SBC tracks follow the same safety model.

Choose a validated targetDeployment begins from known hardware and firmware knowledge rather than a name-only guess.
Acquire and write firmwarePrepare replacement media using the firmware appropriate to the selected target.
Complete first-boot provisioningApply supported network, identity, storage-expansion and controller settings needed by that firmware.
Deploy the complete selected system contentROMs, BIOS, artwork/media and supported user data are routed into the firmware's native locations.
Verify deployed contentRLB retains proof of completed work so successful copy and verification stages do not need to be repeated unnecessarily.
Finalize the frontendRefresh or rebuild frontend data, enable local artwork where appropriate, and complete the firmware-specific handoff.
